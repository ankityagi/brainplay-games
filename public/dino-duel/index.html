Dino Duel

Pick a dinosaur, land your hits, climb 10 levels.

How to win. Every hit you land drains the other dino's health bar. When it runs out, that dino is knocked out. Win 2 rounds to win the fight.
Block to stop hits. Your Power bar fills up as you fight. When it glows, fire your dino's super move.
Player 1: A D move, W jump, S block, F hit, G power move.
Player 2: ← → move, ↑ jump, ↓ block, K hit, L power move.
